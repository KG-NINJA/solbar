A 2025 report from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) reveals that $28.8 billion was paid in improper claims due to coding errors and insufficient documentation.

## 🎯 Core Problem
The "Documentation-to-Code Integrity Gap." Physicians select billing codes based on clinical complexity, but their notes often lack the specific regulatory "evidence" required to survive an audit.

## 👤 Affected Users
Healthcare providers, medical billing departments, and compliance officers.

## 💥 Pain Point
"Revenue Leakage & Audit Anxiety." Retroactive claim denials create high-stress environments and financial instability.

## 🔍 Hidden Opportunity
Real-time "ambient clinical nudge systems" that bridge the gap *during* the documentation process.

## 💡 Solution Direction
An AI-powered "Documentation Guardian" integrated into the EHR to cross-reference CPT codes against draft notes in real-time.

## 💰 Monetization Idea
"Found Revenue" Share or per-provider SaaS subscription.
